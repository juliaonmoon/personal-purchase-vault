# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Validation
Landing page, clickable flow, demand and willingness-to-pay tests.

## Phase 1 — Core Vault
Auth, upload, extraction, editable purchase/items, secure documents, search.

## Phase 2 — Deadlines
Returns, warranties, reminder engine, dashboard.

## Phase 3 — Inventory
Automatic/manual inventory, categories, rooms, tags, model/serial data.

## Phase 4 — Subscriptions
Recurring service records, trials, renewals, reminders.

## Phase 5 — Monetization
Free limits, paid plans, billing, usage/storage controls.

## Phase 6 — Intelligence
Policy lookup, claim guidance, price protection, additional money-saving automation.
